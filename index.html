GOATED VIDEO GAMES

Manhunt, created by Rockstar Games, is a stealth base horror title that became very popular for its raw intensity and unsettling atmoshphere. originally it was launched for Playstation 2, Xbox, and PC, and later it would be re-released digilally for PS4-PS5. In the game you play as James Earl Cash, a death row inmate whos is forced to film a series of snuff films that is filmed by a twisteddirector where gangsters are sent to kill James Earl Cash. The games strengths comes from its slow burn tension, gritty enviornments, and the constant ned to hide, sneak and outsmart your enemies rather than to rush into combat. Its sound design, shadowy locations, and sense of being hunted creates a heavy nerve wracking mood that sticks with players even when theyre not playing the game.

In this game you you follow a news crew thats investigating the abondened home of a notorious serial killer known as the easter ripper, only to figure out that he may not be as dead as everyone thought. The game has grainy visuals, and puts you on edge the entire game which makes you feel as if youre never safe, it has a tense at atmoshphere and a scary cat and mouse gameplay. The gameplay is also accompanied with a soundtrack and sound design that sounds like an old schoolhorror vibe. Murder house is available on PC, Playstation, Xbox, and Nintendo, making it very easy to play in every platform.
